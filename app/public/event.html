How to read this Placement is out of the event's entrants. W-L counts sets won and lost at that event, with DQs excluded. Major columns count this game only, using the main bracket (largest field). Series columns are the series the most registrants played: best placement (with its W-L), best W-L, and average W-L when there are 2+ events. Seed and notes are suggestions. 📌 pins a row under the header; ❄ freezes a column. Click a player for their tables; click a column header in those tables to sort.
Compares placements at the same events. Not head-to-head sets.
