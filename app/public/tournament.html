How to read this
Placement is out of the event's entrants. W-L counts sets won and lost at that event, with DQs excluded. Major columns count this game only, using the main bracket (largest field). Series columns are the series the most registrants played: best placement (with its W-L), best W-L, and average W-L when there are 2+ events. Click a column header to sort; ❄ freezes that column; 📌 pins a row under the header. Tick up to 3 rows to compare them. Double-click Tier or Notes to edit (Enter saves, Esc cancels). Click a player for their tables; click a column header in those tables to sort.
